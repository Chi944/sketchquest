# SketchQuest product context

Source of truth: the user's approved build brief and implementation request. This records known context; it is not new audience research.

SketchQuest lets someone photograph a small drawn puzzle, review and correct its interpretation, play it, request a bounded change in words, inspect the changed cells and deterministic proof, then accept and share it. A curious first-time visitor on a laptop or phone is an audience assumption, not a tested demographic.

The board is the primary experience. A complete manual path remains usable without AI credentials. The memorable demonstration is a proposed edit that provably increases the shortest solution, followed by replay. A longer shortest path is a measurable proxy, not a claim about human difficulty.

Only the documented 4–8 cell grid game ships. No accounts, galleries, multiplayer, arbitrary model-generated code, or paid model fallback. Original photographs never become part of shared snapshots. Every accepted change preserves the previous board revision and starts a fresh play session.

Engineering success means shared pure rules, complete-state BFS, honest solved/unsolvable/inconclusive outcomes, replay-checked solutions, stale-result protection, recoverable editing, and server-validated immutable sharing. Product success means the current board state and next action are clear on keyboard and touch.

The repository includes authored synthetic evaluation drawings. Real-photo model quality and production deployment are not yet measured or provisioned. They must remain explicit limitations.
